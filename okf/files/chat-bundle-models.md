A RAM tier per lineage: the 27B rungs by installed gigabytes, one gemma per
phone above tier 3 and the ternary 1.7B at or below it, plus everything
already downloaded so a set on disk never vanishes from the picker.
`installedGB` is the one rounding the tiers and the speech floor share, so
they cannot disagree about what a device is.
